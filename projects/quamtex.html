In use

Verifiable case covering four solutions: ordering, prospecting, collections and voice-based lead qualification.

The need.

Quamtex needed to connect four commercial workflows previously handled as separate tasks: order intake, prospect research, collections follow-up and lead qualification by phone.

KATIA’s approach.

KATIA organized the case into four solutions with clear ownership and human review. Order Intake is in use, Lead Finder operates as an ongoing service, and the Collections and Voice entries document their scope within commercial operations.
