Before you decide.

Does this include Amazon and other marketplaces?

Each channel is evaluated separately. Marketplace access and rules determine what can be read or updated. The proposal names the included channels.

How are duplicate orders or updates handled?

The design includes agreed identifiers, states and retry rules. Duplicates and exceptions are tested before adoption; final controls depend on scope.
