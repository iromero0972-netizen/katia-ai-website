What you need to know before taking the next step.

Questions, pricing and calculator
Where do we start?

With a free 30-minute consultation. We review a process, identify a concrete opportunity and define the next step. You do not need technical expertise or to make a purchasing decision on the call.

Does it integrate with the tools we use?

We first review your systems, permissions and available APIs. We can design integrations with accounting tools, ERP, CRM, payments and e-commerce. Compatibility, licenses and third-party costs are confirmed in the proposal.

Does my team need AI expertise?

No. We design around how your team works and include training, documentation and support according to scope. We start with one useful process and expand after validation.

Does KATIA replace my accountant or tax advisor?

KATIA organizes information and automates workflows to support their work. Accounting records, reports and tax documents require review by the responsible professionals. Tax advice, representation and filing are not included in these technology services.

What is the investment?

Each solution is priced according to scope. These are the published reference prices for Control plans; your proposal confirms workflows, support, integrations and additional costs.
