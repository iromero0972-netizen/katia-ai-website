Before you decide.

Can the app work on iOS, Android and the web?

Yes. The platform combination depends on users, features and distribution. Web apps and apps distributed through stores have different requirements and release processes.

Does this include Apple and Google store publication?

It is included when specified in the proposal. It requires accounts, documentation, testing and each store’s review. A demo design does not guarantee approval.

Can it connect to inventory or accounting software?

We assess permissions, documentation and system APIs. Features, costs and responsibilities are documented before committing to an integration.
