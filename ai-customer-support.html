Before you decide.

Can I speak with a KATIA agent?

Yes. Leo is available by phone and Andrés on WhatsApp. Use this page’s links to explore those channels. The setup for your business is defined separately.

Can an agent approve discounts or financial decisions?

Sensitive decisions stay with the responsible people. We define permissions, limits and escalation criteria before activating actions in business systems.

Can it support Amazon customers?

Features depend on the channel, permissions and available APIs. Our Amazon Service Desk project presents an organization and human-review workflow; it does not imply authorization for automatic replies in every marketplace.
